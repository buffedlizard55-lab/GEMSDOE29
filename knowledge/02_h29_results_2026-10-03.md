## The reported 0.2600 and submission decision

The pinned owner-mirrored parent/D1.5/D2.8 sequence is recorded as 121,131 / 60,069 / 44,090 positive pixels and scores 0.1922 / 0.2477 / 0.2600. The deterministic local `dot_thin(H19-5, 2.8)` reproduction matches the D2.8 **pixel mask** exactly; TIFF bytes differ. The 0.2600 association remains owner-reported; no organizer receipt ties that file to the score. It is not a new candidate and should not be resubmitted.

A plausible geometric explanation—not a causal proof—is score-budget calibration: the official distance-weighted Tversky metric gives continuous credit within a 300 m linear support radius and weights false negatives (0.8) more than false positives (0.2). A 2.8-pixel separation on the 100 m grid is 280 m; along an ideal straight trace the midpoint between adjacent dots is roughly 140 m from one, retaining about 0.53 of linearly weighted credit while pruning redundant or remote pixels. This is consistent with the reported score trend but cannot establish score-to-file association or performance on secret labels.
