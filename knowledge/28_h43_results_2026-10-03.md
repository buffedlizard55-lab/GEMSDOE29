## 2. Scientific reading of the four arms

1. **Why `A4_h43_union` (`+0.0039976`) is the strongest non-G1 result in the repository and why it still
   fails.** `A4_h43_union` cleared *every* stability, floor, budget, and sparsity criterion in G1:
   - Positive mean gain in **all four spatial quadrants** (`NW +0.003873`, `NE +0.003499`, `SW +0.006423`,
     `SE +0.002196`), with a **positive worst-fold gain (`+0.0021960` vs the `−0.010` floor)**.
   - Positive in **3 of 4 folds on draw 32** and **4 of 4 folds on draw 33** (`min = 3 >= 3`).
   - Holdout ROC-AUC increased from `0.78829` (`C0_base`) to `0.79127` (`+0.00298`), with emitted dot counts
     tightly held inside `0.9881–1.0149×` control.
   - It failed G1 solely because its 8-cell mean paired gain (`+0.0039976`) is `0.0010024` below the frozen
     `+0.00500` effect-size bar. Under the project's pre-commitment rule, a `+0.0040` gain against a
     `+0.0050` gate is a **clean fail**, never rounded up or re-gated after seeing the cells.
2. **Interaction between catchment size (`LNACC, OMEGA`) and knickpoint excess (`KNICK, OFF_FRONT`).**
   Looking across the four arms explains the mechanism:
   - `A1_h43_off_front` (`H43_OFF_FRONT, H43_KNICK` alone) loses `−0.0011044` (`2/4` folds positive on both
     draws): a log-slope residual above the basin envelope, given to the boosted trees *without* catchment
     area `A` or stream power `Omega`, cannot distinguish a steep anomaly on a major range-front trunk
     channel (`A ~ 10^4` cells) from a noisy headwater step right at the `A = 25` cutoff.
   - `A2_h43_omega_area` (`H43_LNACC, H43_OMEGA, H43_CHANNEL_SCARP`, no knickpoint residual) gains
     `+0.0018808` (`3/4` folds on both draws, worst fold `−0.0003184`).
   - Combining both blocks in `A4_h43_union` more than doubles `A2`'s gain to `+0.0039976` and turns every
     quadrant mean positive (`+0.00220` to `+0.00642`): the tree needs `H43_LNACC`/`H43_OMEGA` to condition
     on drainage order *and* `H43_KNICK`/`H43_OFF_FRONT`/`H43_CHANNEL_SCARP` to locate where that channel
     crosses an active structural step.
3. **`A3_h43_scarp_free` is the first feature arm to pass the SGMC secondary-proxy fold gate while gaining
   on the primary proxy.** Unlike H41 (where every arm moved the SGMC off-catalogue class negative on 3/4
   folds), `A3_h43_scarp_free` (`LNACC, OMEGA, KNICK, OFF_FRONT`, omitting `H43_CHANNEL_SCARP`) achieved
   **`+0.0013560` mean SGMC gain with `3/4` folds positive** (`NW +0.006825`, `NE +0.001607`, `SW +0.003914`,
   `SE −0.006921`, `sgmc_gate_pass = True`) alongside a `+0.0019365` primary gain and `+0.00245` AUC lift.
   Conversely, adding `H43_CHANNEL_SCARP` (`Omega * h27_scarp`) in `A4_h43_union` doubles the catalogue-hidden
   gain (`+0.00194 -> +0.00400`) at the cost of pulling the SGMC gain back to `−0.001125` (`2/4` folds),
   because `h27_scarp` pulls dots toward scarp-bearing range fronts that match the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue
   style rather than 1:250k bedrock contacts.
