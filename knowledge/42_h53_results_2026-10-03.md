## 1. What was tested

Three label-free multi-scale DEM orientation columns built by `src/gemsdoe/h53.py` from
`12_det_elev.npy` (structure tensor at scales 3 / 7 / 15 px = 300 / 700 / 1500 m, σ_grad 1.0 px,
σ_tensor = 1.0·scale, τ = 15°, coherence ≥ 0.15, ≥ 1e-3 of each scale's own p99 |∇|, 30 px high-pass):
`H53_COH_MIN`, `H53_AGREE` (coherence-weighted axial resultant, mod 180°), `H53_NSCALES`, and
`H53_PERSIST = COH_MIN × AGREE`. Arms (declared primary in bold):

| arm | columns added to the H34 feature set | what it asks |
|---|---|---|
| `C0_base` | — (control) | frozen `score_ordered_dots` reference |
| `C1_geodesic_dots` | — (control) | the recorded slot bar, `dot_thin` |
| **`A1_h53_persist`** | 4 `H53_*` columns | does scale-persistent fabric add **recall**? |
| `A2_h53_off` | the same 4 columns restricted to the off-catalogue mask | does the *effect* help where the catalogue is absent? |
| `A3_h53_scarp` | `H53_PERSIST` × `norm(H27_scarp)` interaction | does fabric sharpen the existing scarp column? |

Arm columns are appended after the 64 static + 5 add-on + 7 extra + 3 H27 + H30 columns (85 features per
cell before the arm's own columns). "Off" is the same field zeroed inside the dilated catalogue
(`OFF_CATALOGUE_MIN_PX = 5` px, declared in the frozen design), so `A2` cannot leak catalogue
proximity along the mask boundary.

## 2. Execution record (read before the numbers)

* **Two processes under one frozen design.** The launch (`--phase all`, commit `48e200e`) froze
  `design.json` and completed the 8 control cells (16 rows) plus 3 of the 8 screen cells
  (`NW d36`, `NW d37`, `NE d36`) before the container **OOM-killed it (exit 137)**. The stage was closed out
  with `--phase screen --resume` (commit `685de4e`), which skips cells already in the append-only
  `cells.jsonl`. The 5 remaining screen cells wrote the last 25 rows; the runner reported
  `phase rows written: 56`. No row was deleted, rewritten or re-ordered at any point.
* **The resumed process ran under a later commit than the one `design.json` records.** `design.json` holds
  the **launch** revision `48e200e` (the runner deliberately keeps the launch design on resume). The resume
  ran at `685de4e`, a commit that only added `scripts/calibrate_proxy_against_reported_scores.py` and its
  evidence file — but whose message does not say so, and which also carried the then-partial `cells.jsonl`
  as an unmentioned passenger. Disclosed as `IR-29-H53-COMMIT-PASSENGER`.
* **The evidence is pinned to the code by hash, not by revision.** `design.json` records the SHA-256 of the
  five modules that determine a cell and of the two input caches. What the runner *enforced* at resume was
  the frozen **preregistration** hash only; the module and cache digests were recorded at launch and are
  re-checked by the analyzer at close-out (and were re-checked again after the sandbox reset). All five
  module hashes and the `knowledge/41` preregistration hash **match the current tree byte-for-byte**
  (§7). Per-row revisions are not recorded, so the process split is proven by the append-only cell file,
  not by cell metadata.
* **A cache-sidecar hash check produced a false integrity flag after the reset, and was fixed.**
  `data/work/h53_fields.npy` rebuilt **byte-identical** (`bdf67f9f12a57d04…`), but the sidecar
  `h53_fields.json` differs because it stores `generated_utc` and `elapsed_s`; the analyzer's original
  digest comparison therefore reported "field cache changed or missing" even though `config` and
  `diagnostics` were object-equal. The analyzer now compares the `.npy` byte-for-byte and the sidecar on its
  substantive fields — strictly stronger than the digest it replaces. (`IR-29-H53-SIDECAR-HASH`.) The
  equivalent hardening of the runner's `--resume` guard was drafted and deliberately **not** applied:
  `scripts/run_h53_screen.py` carries a byte-for-byte hash pin inside the frozen `design.json`, so editing
  it now would break the very pin this stage relies on. It is recorded as follow-up for the next frozen
  design.
* **The numbers were seen once before the official run finished** — in a scratch smoke run
  (`--out /tmp/h53_smoke`, 40 cells, 915 s, same code revision and same frozen design) whose output was
  never written under `evidence/`. Its 40 per-cell DTI values agree with the official run to the digits
  printed in §3 (seeds are deterministic). Disclosed as `IR-29-H53-SCRATCH-PREVIEW`; the analyzer report is
  the authority.
* **This close-out was written after a sandbox reset** that re-cloned the repository and dropped the
  session's local commits while keeping the working tree. Every artifact referenced here was re-verified on
  disk before being re-committed (§7). Disclosed as `IR-29-SANDBOX-GIT-RESET`.

The OOM is a known container property of this protocol: each cell holds the full 85-column training matrix
(≈150 k rows × 4 bytes), and a cell costs ≈100 s. Chunked single-phase runs (`--resume`) are the documented
operating mode — the same lesson H43 recorded as `IR-29-H43-STAGED-EXECUTION`.

## 5. Reading: what H53 did and did not establish

1. **The columns are real and not inert.** They are dense (95.7 % nonzero), they were chosen by a frozen
   rule on a spent draw, their single-column off-catalogue design statistic was the best in the audit
   (`H53_PERSIST` AUC **0.5933** vs 0.4944–0.5064 for five incumbents, `evidence/h53_design_audit.json`),
   and the model uses them heavily (up to 116 splits in one cell). This is not a missing-column or
   dead-column failure.
2. **The design statistic did not convert into credit.** The audit's 0.5933 was measured on *all* footprint
   pixels; the screen measures only the credit a **binary dot emission** earns inside a hidden quadrant,
   after thinning at 2.4 px. The primary arm's mean gain (+0.00156) sits at the very bottom of the frozen
   honest prior (+0.001 to +0.004, `knowledge/41` §7) and far below the +0.005 bar. So: the fabric column
   ranks hidden faults *slightly* better than the incumbents pixel-wise, but the extra ranking does not
   survive the ordered-dot emission — the same "design statistic ≠ emission gain" gap H31/H35/H41/H52 hit.
3. **`A2` is the interesting negative, and it is the third instance of one pattern.** Restricting the same
   columns to the off-catalogue mask is the *best* arm on the primary proxy (+0.008457, 2/4 and 4/4
   positive folds, ≈0.0045 below the bar in mean DTI) and it is **negative on the SGMC proxy on all four
   folds** (−0.007591). H41's union arm and H43's knick arm had exactly this shape (positive on the primary
   proxy, G1-pass, negative SGMC). The inherited G3 rule withholds promotion, so `A2` is not built.
   `IR-29-PROXY-VETO-PATTERN` now has three data points; §6 of `knowledge/43` calibrates both proxies
   against the ten owner-reported sibling scores and shows the SGMC proxy is the *worse* ranking device on
   those files.
4. **What would have to change for H53 to matter.** Either (a) the fabric column is right but the emission
   is the bottleneck — then the fix belongs to a *shared* emission study, not to another H53 arm; or (b) the
   fabric field is only informative where it is diluted (the ±600 m fault band). Both were declared
   out-of-scope in `knowledge/41` §8 and are *not* retro-fitted here. H53 stays screened/closed.
