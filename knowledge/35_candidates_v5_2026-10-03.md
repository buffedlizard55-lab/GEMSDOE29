## Rank 1 — H50: range-front segmentation (mountain-front sinuosity per front segment)

- **Layers:** `det_elev` + `det_elev_slope` (cached 100 m bands), family-B curvature for front
  extraction, the supplied catalogue only for the off-catalogue mask.
- **Physical signature:** the mountain-front sinuosity index Smf = Lmf / Ls (along-front length over
  straight-line length) computed per range-front segment, plus segment-scale relief. Straight,
  high-relief segments (Smf ≈ 1.0–1.4) are fault-controlled active fronts; embayed low-relief segments
  (Smf ≫ 1.4) are erosional. Feature = fault-controlled-front likelihood concentrated on straight
  segments that carry **no** catalogue trace (an unmapped strand holding the front up), delivered with a
  segment-validity mask. (Citation to verify at preregistration: the Smf index is the standard
  Bull/McFadden tectonic-geomorphology measure — confirm the textbook reference before quoting it as a
  source; the formula above is the complete specification.)
- **Why off-catalogue:** the catalogue maps strands, not front activity. A straight 5–15 km front with
  no mapped trace is exactly "newly mapped geometry of an existing system": the hidden labels add the
  strand the front shape already implies. Local-window scarp filters (B/L columns) see the step but not
  the *segment*; H43's knickpoints see the drainage response but not the front planform.
- **Difference from repo work:** no range-front, sinuosity, Smf or faceted-spur computation exists
  anywhere in `src/`, `scripts/` or `tests/` (session-6 grep: zero hits). H27/H30 filter scarp *pixels*;
  H35 pairs catalogue tips; H43 routes water. H50 is the first *segment-scale tectonic-geomorphology*
  index: its unit is the front, not the pixel or the catchment.
- **Cost / rank justification:** zero downloads, ~0.5 day (front extraction + Smf + mask + synthetic
  tests). Expected gain bracket 0 to +0.012 — the mechanism (fronts ARE the fault habitat) is the most
  on-label in the slate, and segment-scale structure is the largest spatial scale the project has not
  yet tested. Ranked first.
- **Known risks:** 100 m DEM coarsens short fronts; pediment/volcanic-cover fronts have no meaningful
  Smf (hence the validity mask); Smf correlates with lithology as well as activity, so the arm design
  must isolate the off-catalogue straight-segment increment.

## Rank 2 — H49: radiometric alteration corridors (K-enrichment anomaly objects)

- **Layers:** `geodawn_rad_u8` (K, Th, U, TC) + `geodawn_extensions_u8` (Th/K, U/K, U/Th, TMI_up150),
  both already mirrored; off-catalogue mask.
- **Physical signature:** thresholded K-enrichment / Th-normalized anomaly *objects* (connected
  K/Th-high, U-textured zones = hydrothermal clay alteration halos) skeletonized to corridors, instead
  of raw ratio pixels. Potassic alteration marks paleo-fluid pathways; a corridor with no mapped trace
  is a covered fault that once plumbed fluids.
- **Why off-catalogue:** alteration halos survive burial and cover that erase scarps and mute
  magnetics; the supplied catalogue has no alteration channel, and the D-family use of these bands is
  raw-ratio pixels, which a boosted tree reads as texture, not as bounded halos.
- **Difference from repo work:** the only "alteration" hit in `src/` is a one-line comment in
  `families.py` placing MT conductance in family D; no K-enrichment thresholding, halo-object building
  or corridor skeletonization exists in `src/`, `scripts/` or `tests/`. H35 used D_ThK/D_UK as raw
  corroboration pixels; H49 tests *objects*, and the arm design isolates object-ness from ratio-ness.
- **Cost / rank justification:** zero downloads, ~0.5–1 day. Bracket 0 to +0.010. Ranked second: the
  physics is second-order (pathway, not structure) but the fields are dense and the build is cheap.
- **Known risks:** radiometrics sense the top ~30–50 cm, so "alteration" competes with soil/lithology
  noise; the threshold needs a frozen percentile rule declared before any fit.

## Rank 3 — H51: H41×H43 cross-family agreement (conjunction of two vetoed-but-replicated fields)

- **Layers:** no new layers — the five frozen H41 columns (`src/gemsdoe/h41.py`) and the five frozen
  H43 columns (`src/gemsdoe/h43.py`), both already implemented, tested and screened.
- **Physical signature:** per-pixel *agreement* between two independent structural priors that share no
  input (INGENIOUS slip-rate corridors vs drainage-network knickpoints): normalized rank-agreement and
  a both-high conjunction mask, restricted off-catalogue. Two unrelated fields agreeing on the same
  unmapped line is stronger evidence than either alone — and the SGMC veto pattern (`knowledge/32`: 0
  of 5 gate-passing arm-stages SGMC-positive) makes the conjunction the cheapest test of whether
  agreement *resolves* the proxy conflict or inherits it.
- **Why off-catalogue:** both parents are already off-catalogue-restricted; the conjunction inherits the
  restriction. It catches faults neither family places alone only insofar as agreement re-ranks shared
  corridors above single-family noise — the honest ceiling is re-ranking, not new mass.
- **Difference from repo work:** every union tested so far (H35 A2, H41 A4, H43 A4) concatenates columns
  inside one model. No screen has tested an explicit cross-family *agreement field* as its own arm, and
  none has used the SGMC-conflict question as the design criterion. H51 is a methods experiment with a
  geological payoff, and it is the only slate item that can *shrink* the veto problem instead of
  routing around it.
- **Cost / rank justification:** zero downloads, ~0.5 day (fields exist; only the agreement transform +
  screen are new). Bracket −0.005 to +0.010 — the only slate item with a negative lower bound, because
  both parents are vetoed and the conjunction can inherit both vetoes. Ranked third: cheapest
  information-per-cell about the project's central open question, but not new physics.
- **Known risks:** draw hygiene — H41 fitted draws 20/21 + 28–31, H43 fitted 32–35, so the screen needs
  the next free pair (36/37, `registry/draw_ledger.json` is the authority) and must never touch a spent
  draw for either parent's columns.

## Rank 4 — H48: volcanic-vent / paleo-geothermal alignment corridors

- **Layers:** `ext_gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv` (already mirrored, 672 bytes — a handful of
  vents), plus `paleo_geothermal_regional.zip` and `great_basin_q_volcanics.zip` from GDR 1391
  (NOT mirrored; exact URLs + pins recorded in `data/manifest.json` → `blocked_this_session`).
- **Physical signature:** vent/paleo-site kernel density plus alignment corridors (structure-tensor /
  Hough orientations of the point sets): vents mark permeable crustal pathways, and *aligned* vents
  mark the fault that plumbed them.
- **Why off-catalogue:** vents and paleo-sites are point observations independent of fault mapping; an
  alignment with no mapped trace is a blind structure with a surface fluid witness.
- **Difference from repo work:** `vents_in_footprint`, `volcanic vent` and `paleo` return zero hits in
  `src/`, `scripts/` and `tests/` — the mirrored vents CSV has never been read as a predictor, and the
  paleo/volcanics archives have never been fetched here. H37 works spring *chemistry*; D_* works
  well/spring *density*; nothing works vent *alignments*.
- **Cost / rank justification:** 1–2 days *after* an owner-side fetch; the mirrored-vents-only subset
  could be screened immediately but its ceiling is tiny (few points → sparse field, the H31 failure
