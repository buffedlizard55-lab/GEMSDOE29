## 3. Reading the negative result

1. **The H41 union's apparent gain was draw-specific.** On draws 28/29 it measured +0.0073436 and on 30/31
   +0.0077283 (screen and confirmation, `knowledge/26`); on draws 20/21 the same arm, same columns, same
   emission measures **+0.001183**. Same sign, one fifth of the size, mean below the +0.005 bar. The honest
   summary is that the family's earlier passes were consistent with a small positive effect that this stage
   cannot separate from draw noise at the frozen effect size.
2. **Both protocols agree about the fold that breaks it.** SW is negative in the screen (−0.0016554) and here
   (−0.009202); the draw-level "3 of 4 folds positive" pattern that let `A4` pass G1 twice is the same pattern
   that fails to produce a mean large enough on a third independent draw pair. This is the fold-concentration
   criticism `knowledge/22` §4 raised, now measured on the bar protocol.
3. **The secondary proxy is worse here, not better.** SGMC loses on all four folds (−0.0037 to −0.0095), where
   the H41 screen had 1/4 folds positive and a mean of −0.0023. Both proxies therefore fail together on these
   draws: the primary by effect size, the secondary by sign. The registered proxy conflict
   (`IR-29-PROXY-CONFLICT`) is not resolved in H41's favour by any measurement in this repository.
4. **The bar itself is intact.** `C1_geodesic_dots` (0.14479) remains the best recorded method outcome in this
   project on any protocol, and the H41 union is 0.0012 above it on the same cells with a mean gain that does
   not reach the frozen effect size. There is no slot-eligible method in this repository, and this stage did
   not create one.

## 5. What is now known, and what would have to change to re-open this line

- A re-opening would need a *mechanism* change, not another draw pair on the same five columns: the INGENIOUS
  centroid mirror carries one point per trace and no polyline (`knowledge/26` §3), so the ceiling of this
  feature family is km-scale prior shaping; the observed effect is ~0.001–0.008 depending on draws, i.e. at or
  below the noise floor of the spatial folds. A candidate that wants a slot has to clear +0.005 on the bar
  protocol *and* move the secondary proxy, and nothing in the worming/H41 family has done either.
- Draws 20/21 are now spent on three arms beyond the H34 controls; the H41 columns have been seen on them, so
  they can never again serve as a fresh test for this family. That is recorded here rather than discovered
  later.
