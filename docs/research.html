Rank 1 · H32

Asymmetric fault-scarp cross-profile shape

unimplemented; first candidate for a new preregistration after the main-branch review

Layers: L_step_max, L_cross_max, L_downface_max, L_upface_max, L_coh100, detrended elevation, detrended slope

Physical signature: Compare opposing-face steepness and width at a coherent terrain step; a one-sided scarp may be more fault-like than a symmetric channel bank, ridge or bench.

Why it could add unmapped faults: Fault scarps may retain terrain expression even when USGS/INGENIOUS trace layers omit the fault.

How it differs from reviewed work: At GEMSDOE25 commit efc4be7bb3cfdf2def12a7fb83284d3311ac9220, the code dequantizes downface_max/upface_max as separate inputs; H30-1 uses a step × cross-profile × coherence composite and H30-3 registers cross-profile scale persistence. The raw 1-m scarp-template idea is deferred. A repository-wide search of code, tests, hypotheses and knowledge at that commit found no normalized opposing-face asymmetry ratio. H32 tests that specific derived shape ratio, not another scarp-magnitude product.

Rank 2 · H31

Regularized pseudogravity transform and edge-drift increment

prototype implemented; preregistered; no model fit; priority reduced after H29 result

Layers: rtp, iso_grav_anom

Physical signature: Apply a documented low-wavenumber-regularized vertical-integration pseudogravity proxy to RTP, track horizontal-gradient maxima across the fixed continuation ladder, and test lateral drift separately from persistence; include a symmetric 200-m cross-field support term.

Why it could add unmapped faults: A buried susceptibility boundary may yield a coherent gravity-equivalent edge and scale-dependent lateral path where no surface trace is mapped in USGS/INGENIOUS; this is a structural hypothesis, not a depth/fault proof.

H31 tests only a different magnetic transform (regularized vertical integration before continuation) and an explicit drift feature. It must not be presented as a first test of worming or persistence.

Rank 1 · H35

Structural-interaction-zone field with dilation-tendency weighting

specified; not implemented; ranked first for the next preregistration

Layers: visible-catalogue geometry (labels/existing_faults), L_step_max/L_cross_max/L_coh100/L_strike_c2/s2, A_mag_hg_ridge/A_grav_hg_ridge, geod_shearrate/geod_dilaterate/geod_2ndinv, D_ThK/D_UK

Physical signature: Explicit interaction-zone objects: overlapping sub-parallel trace tips inside a 0.5-3 km relay window, terminations/horse-tails, and normal x transverse intersections; each weighted by dilation tendency from the regional stress field and corroborated by an independent field (scarp step+coherence, potential-field ridge, or radiometric contrast).

Why it could add unmapped faults: Step-overs/relay ramps host 32-47% of Great Basin geothermal systems and terminations 22-25%, while range-front faults host ~1% (Faulds & Hinz 2015; Gidden & Faulds 2025). Catalogue mapping follows through-going strands and systematically misses short overlapping strands - the geometry the organizer says the hidden labels add.

How it differs from reviewed work: Family E is distance/orientation/density around visible faults; H27_tip is a single-tip continuation; H30-1 (paired-tip bridge x scarp) failed fresh-draw confirmation. H35 pairs tips inside a relay window, weights by dilation tendency rather than distance-to-trace, and adds transverse-intersection objects.

Cost: about 1-2 days

Data: optional USGS slip/dilation-tendency shapefile (Siler 2022, DOI 10.5066/P9YL58W6); metadata verified, sciencebase.gov unreachable from the sandbox, so the hypothesis is written to run without it

H31 research design

Test the pseudogravity/drift increment beyond H29

H29 already ran upward-continuation worm persistence on raw RTP and isostatic gravity, including joint persistence as gate/rank/head features. No arm passed the preregistered +0.005 screen. Draws 2–3 were computed for all arms despite screen failures, so the reconciliation treats them as exploratory extras rather than eligible confirmations. H31 does not claim worming itself is new. It isolates a regularized vertical-integration pseudogravity proxy from RTP plus a lateral edge-drift feature, then checks whether those additions improve a same-run baseline. The available isostatic gravity anomaly is included separately. A symmetric Euclidean 200-m cross-support (5×5 bounding window, diagonal corners excluded) allows small grid misregistration; it is a tolerance, not geological proof.

The first synthetic engineering test found exact-pixel multiplication produced an all-zero joint term after the distinct transforms. Before any real-data fit, the preregistered joint feature was clarified to use the fixed ±2-pixel tolerance; no model, data, scale, or gate changed.

Current screen: H34: FAIL on the catalogue-hidden proxy (mean paired gain -0.0212 vs best control, 0/4 folds positive), PASS on the SGMC off-catalogue proxy (+0.0535, 4/4 folds); H29: no arm passed; H31: screen not run. Confirmation is H34 is negative on its primary proxy and therefore not eligible for confirmation; H31 confirmation remains blocked until a screen passes.

Scientific limits

Worming-like ≠ full inversion

Poisson-wavelet worming literature motivates upward continuation and tracking horizontal-gradient maxima. The implementation here is a scale-space proxy with regularized Fourier integration, not the full Hornby transform, not an inversion, and not a fault-depth estimator.

Potential fields are non-unique: source interference, cultural noise, depth, remanence and misalignment can produce or hide edges. Persistence is not proof of faulting, geothermal activity, or discovery.

Promotion gates

Spatial validation before any slot

Four spatial quadrants are the replication units; folds and draws are paired cells, not independent extra samples. H31 must beat the strongest same-run control by a paired mean DTI gain above 0.001, be positive in at least 3/4 spatial blocks, never lose more than 0.010 in one block, and avoid a material increase in catalogue-hug share. A fresh two-draw confirmation with the same gates is required. Raw-cell hashes and screen gates are recomputed before confirmation.

Passing these gates only permits a candidate to be considered; it does not authorize a weekly submission, guarantee the official score, or establish generalization to expert-labelled faults.
